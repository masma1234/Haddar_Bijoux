# Legal review checklist

This is a checklist for a qualified lawyer assuming a Tunisian business. It is not legal advice or a statement that the draft is legally sufficient. Sources checked 13 October 2026.

- Organic Law 2004-63 on personal data protection: identify the controller, lawful purposes, rights contact, retention, hosting/Instagram recipients and transfers, and any INPDP formalities. Review the [INPDP legal texts](https://www.inpdp.tn/Texte.html) and [the law](https://www.inpdp.tn/ressources/loi_2004.pdf).
- Law 2000-83 on electronic exchanges and e-commerce: review precontractual information, ordering through Instagram, proof/confirmation, payment, delivery, withdrawal and refunds against the actual sales process. The [Ministry of Communication Technologies text](https://www.mtc.gov.tn/fileadmin/texte_juridiques/L2000-0083.pdf) is the source text.
- Consumer protection law 92-117: review seller identity, product information, safety, advertising, pricing and warranties. Both this law and Law 2000-83 are listed in the [Ministry of Commerce's e-commerce legal framework](https://commerce.gov.tn/presentation-de-lenvironnement-du-e-commerce-en-tunisie_11_187).
- GDPR: assess territorial applicability under Article 3, including any actual targeting of people in the EU or relevant monitoring. Mere accessibility from the EU is not the same as establishing applicability. Consult the [official GDPR text](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679).
- Confirm whether jewelry, personalized pieces or matching pairs require specific information or exceptions. Do not assume that a personalized piece is excluded from every right.
- Review the client-stated seven-day return window measured from payment. Confirm that the commercial policy does not reduce applicable statutory rights; assess timing, conditions, shipping and reimbursement separately.
- Confirm what the warranty certificate covers and whether statutory warranties need separate wording.
- Confirm Haddar_Bijoux's legal form, RNE, tax identifier and address, or any legitimately inapplicable fields. Do not replace missing registration information with invented numbers.
- Review the actual Cloudflare agreement, logs, processing location and retention. Instagram handles messaging and transactions separately; the website's no-cookie design does not resolve that separate processing.
- Approve the French legal pages first. Only then commission and approve Arabic legal translations. The Arabic footer currently links to French and explains this in derja.

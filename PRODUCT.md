# Haddar Bijoux: product brief
Project: tiny vitrine site for the Instagram page @haddar_bijoux (18-carat gold jewelry). Everything is shown and sold on Instagram, orders by DM. The site only presents the brand and sends people to Instagram. French at `/`, Tunisian Arabic (derja, RTL) at `/ar/`.
Palette (LOCKED): --bg #F7F5F1, --panel #FFFFFF, --ink #2A241E, --ink-soft #6E6459, --gold #B68A4E (thin lines, small icons, primary button fill), --gold-text #7A5A2B (all gold text and links, owner-approved for contrast), --line #E4DCCB (1px hairlines). Light mode only. Never add colors. No beige or tan area fills.
Fonts: Italiana and Jost (FR), Amiri and Tajawal (AR, only on /ar/). Self-hosted.
Bans: gradients, pill-shaped buttons, shadows and glows, custom cursors, scroll animations (one subtle load reveal at most, off under prefers-reduced-motion), fake counters or metrics, vague hero text, drawn or SVG illustrations of jewelry, numbered labels on non-sequential items, em dashes anywhere, "made with AI" or "built with" badges, cookies, analytics, third-party requests.
Facts: never invent business details, reviews, prices, hallmarks, material claims, delivery or return terms. Missing facts become visible [CONFIRMER] markers that block the production build.
Images: only files supplied by the owner in `incoming/`. AI-generated visuals are allowed only as illustrations and must never be described as the owner's real pieces. Never generate or fetch images yourself.
Process: work on a git branch, commit a baseline first, verify before claiming done, report failures plainly.
